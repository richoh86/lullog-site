Lullog: Baby Feeding Tracker

Log every feed, sleep and diaper in three seconds. Share a link, not an app.

Lullog is a newborn log for the 3 AM feed: one hand, two taps, done. The lock screen shows how long since the last feed. Your partner, a grandparent or a sitter opens one link in any browser to see and add entries, with no app to install, no account, and no per-person fee. Pay once for the 7-day summary and charts; logging, widgets and sharing are free forever, with no ads.

What it does

  • Feeds (breast side timer, bottle ml, pumping), sleep and diapers, logged now or "20 minutes ago".
  • Lock screen Live Activity and home screen widgets with time since the last feed and sleep.
  • Works fully offline. Nothing leaves your phone until you turn on sharing.
  • Share a private link with unlimited caregivers. Revoke it any time.
  • Lullog Lifetime, one payment: 7-day summary, daily charts, CSV export.
