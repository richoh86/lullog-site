Frequently asked

I fed the baby twenty minutes ago and forgot to log it.

Tap Feed, then choose 20 min ago at the top of the sheet. You can fine-tune by the minute. Bottle amounts are prefilled from your last bottle.

How do I share the log with my partner?

Settings → Share with caregivers → Connect to sync, then create a link. Send the link by message. It opens in any browser on any phone, with no app and no account. Choose "Can view and log" if they should be able to add entries.

From version 1.3.2 you can also create a limited link. It starts as view only, and you choose whether it may add entries. When you allow recording, a limited link can add entries but cannot edit or delete them. Names and notes stay private on limited links. Links you made earlier keep working as before.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

Not until you tap Connect to sync. Before that, nothing from your log is sent; the app only sends a few anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings. See the privacy policy for exactly what the relay stores once sharing is on.

Do both parents need to pay to share?

No. Sharing, Who logged what and the shared supply list need no purchase. Caregivers open their link in any browser, with no app and no account.

How do I stop sharing or remove someone?

Revoke a single link from the link list, or tap Stop sharing to revoke every link and stop all syncing. Your log stays on your phone either way.

What does Lullog Lifetime include? Is it a subscription?

No subscription. It is one payment, forever, tied to your Apple Account. It unlocks the 7-day summary, daily charts, and CSV export. Logging, the home screen, widgets and sharing are free and stay free.

I bought Lifetime on another phone.

Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is Lullog Plus?

Lullog Plus is a one-time purchase that includes everything in Lullog Lifetime and adds more for everyone looking after the baby: 30-day team trends and a time-of-day view per caregiver, a notification when someone with a link marks a supply Low or Out, three alternative app icons (Night, Apricot, Sage), a growth log, a PDF report of the last 7 or 30 days for the doctor, album tags with a ZIP backup, and a 24-hour pattern of the last 14 days. Find them under More on the Today screen. Logging, sharing, Who logged what (today, this week and the last 7 days) and the shared supply list are free without Plus.

Is there a free trial of Plus?

Yes. On the purchase screen, tap Try Plus for 7 days to unlock every Plus feature for 7 days at no charge. It is a one-time trial, not a subscription: nothing renews and your card is not charged. When the 7 days end, Plus features lock again; your log and everything you made stay, and the free features keep working.

I bought Lifetime. Do I pay full price for Plus?

No. Lifetime owners see a separate upgrade purchase for Plus, priced lower. If the upgrade does not appear, tap Settings → Restore purchase first, signed in with the Apple Account you used for Lifetime.

Is the supply list shared?

Yes, once you have turned sharing on, and it needs no purchase. It syncs through the same relay as your log, so everyone with a link sees it, including on the web share page, and people with a "Can view and log" link can mark items OK, Low or Out. With Lullog Plus you also get a notification when someone with a link marks an item Low or Out; it arrives when Lullog syncs, so when the app is opened or open. With sharing off, the list stays on your iPhone. Stop sharing and delete from server, or Settings → Delete all data, removes it from the server.

Does Lullog interpret growth numbers?

No. Lullog stores the weight, length and head circumference you enter and shows them on simple charts. It does not compare them with charts or tell you whether they are normal. Ask your pediatrician about your baby's growth. Growth entries stay on your iPhone and are never synced.

How do I back up my log and photos?

With Lullog Plus, tap More on the Today screen → Backup. Lullog creates one ZIP file with your log as JSON and CSV files and your photos, then opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it where you like. Restoring from a backup inside the app is not available yet, but the ZIP opens on any computer and the CSV files open in any spreadsheet app.

The lock screen card or widget shows old numbers.

Open the app once; both refresh from the log immediately. The lock screen card ends by itself after eight hours without a new entry.

A sleep crossed midnight. Which day does it count on?

The day it started. This keeps night sleep on the night it belongs to.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data. It asks twice. Export a CSV first if you want a copy. If sharing was connected, it also deletes the shared copy from our server. To delete only the server copy, open Share with caregivers → Stop sharing → Stop sharing and delete from server.

Is Lullog medical advice?

No. Lullog is a logbook. It does not predict sleep or make health claims. Talk to your pediatrician about feeding and sleep concerns.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone. Photos you attach to entries and baby photos you add as moments, with their captions, stay in Lullog's storage on the phone. They are never uploaded, never synced, not part of the share link and not in the CSV export. Removing an entry removes its photo, and Settings → Delete all data removes them all.

Does voice logging send audio anywhere?

No. The microphone button uses Apple's on-device speech recognition only. Audio is not stored and nothing is sent to any server. If on-device recognition is not available for your language, the button is hidden. Lullog asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only when you tap the microphone.
