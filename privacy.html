Privacy Policy

Lullog: Baby Feeding Tracker · Last updated 24 October 2026

한국어

Lullog keeps your baby's log on your phone. Your log leaves the device only if you turn on sharing. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The only other thing the app sends is a few anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your baby's name and date of birth (both optional; you can use "Baby").
  • The entries you log: feeds (breast side, duration, bottle or pumped amount), sleep times, diaper changes, and any note you type.
  • Your settings, such as feeding style and whether you completed onboarding.

This data is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backups. It is not written to iCloud by the app, and the app does not use HealthKit.

Anonymous usage counts (on by default, can be turned off)

From version 1.3.1, Lullog sends a few tiny counters to our own relay server, the same Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 server described under sharing below. We use them only to learn whether new parents manage to save a first entry and come back the next day, so we can improve the app. There is no third-party analytics SDK, no ads and no tracking. The counts are not linked to any account, to your sharing links, or to your baby.

The app sends only these events:

  • That usage counts started on this install, and whether it looked like a fresh install or an existing one.
  • That the first entry was saved, and only whether that happened within 24 hours of starting.
  • Which calendar days (Korea time) the app was opened, and on which days at least one entry was saved.

Each event carries only:

  • A random install ID made only for this purpose. It is not your device ID, and a new one is made if you turn usage counts off and on again.
  • The app version and build number.
  • Calendar dates.

Usage counts never include your baby's name, birthday or age, the contents, times, amounts, counts or notes of any entry, photos, contact details, sharing tokens or links, purchase receipts, your location, or advertising identifiers. Lullog does not use the IDFA.

  • The server does not store IP addresses or user-agents with these events.
  • Raw events are deleted after 30 days. After that we keep only totals, with no install ID.
  • We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for ads.
  • Turning it off: Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. You can turn it off at any time. Lullog then stops sending and deletes any counts still waiting on your phone.

Photos and voice

  • Photos: you can attach a photo to a feed or diaper entry, or add a baby photo as its own moment with a caption. Photos and captions are stored only on your iPhone, in Lullog's private storage (an App Group container that only Lullog and its own extensions can read). They are never uploaded, never synced, never part of a share link, and not included in the CSV export, even when sharing is on. They are included in a ZIP backup only when you create one yourself (see below).
  • Camera and photo library: the camera is used only when you tap Take photo. Choosing an existing picture uses the system photo picker, so Lullog never asks for access to your photo library and sees only the picture you pick.
  • Voice logging: the microphone button uses Apple's on-device speech recognition only. Audio is turned into text on your iPhone, is not stored, and nothing is sent to us or to any server. If on-device recognition is not available for your language, the button is hidden.
  • Permissions: Lullog asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only when you tap the microphone button, and for camera permission only when you tap Take photo.
  • Deleting: removing an entry removes its photo. Settings → Delete all data removes every photo and caption.

Sharing with caregivers (optional, off by default)

Lullog can create a private web link so a partner, grandparent, or sitter can see and add entries from any browser without installing anything. This only happens after you tap Connect to sync in Settings → Share with caregivers. Until then nothing from your log is sent anywhere; the only network requests are the anonymous usage counts described above, if you leave them on.

When sharing is on:

  • Your baby's name, date of birth, feeding style, and the log entries are sent to and stored on our relay server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1.
  • Access is controlled by random, unguessable link tokens. Anyone who has a link can see the log; a link you set to "Can view and log" can also add entries. There are no accounts or passwords.
  • From version 1.3.2 you can also create limited links. A limited link is view only unless you allow it to add entries; it can never edit or delete entries, and it shows less of the log than the earlier kind of link. Links you created before keep the access they had; they are not converted.
  • The server keeps a per-device identifier (a random ID from your iPhone, or the link token for web caregivers) on each entry so devices can merge changes. It stores no email, phone number, or payment data.
  • If you use the shared supply list, its items are sent to and stored on the same relay so every link can show them.
  • You can revoke any link at any time. Stop sharing revokes every link and stops all syncing; your log stays on your phone.

The relay stores only what is needed to show the log to the people you invited. We do not read, sell, or use it for anything else.

Supply list, growth entries, reports and backups

The supply list is included for everyone, with no purchase. Supply alerts, growth entries, album tags, reports and backups are part of Lullog Plus.

  • Supply list: each item has a name (such as Diapers, Wipes or Formula), a status (OK, Low or Out) and an optional note. Items are stored on your iPhone. Only when you have turned sharing on are they synced through our relay on Cloudflare Workers, exactly like the log, so people with a link can see them, including on the web share page, and people with a "Can view and log" link can change them. With sharing off, the list never leaves your phone. The list is deleted from the server together with your log when you choose Stop sharing and delete from server or Settings → Delete all data.
  • Growth entries: weight, length, head circumference and any note you add are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded or synced, even when sharing is on. Lullog shows the numbers you enter and does not interpret them.
  • Album tags: the tags you give photos (Daily, Growth, Documents, Milestone) are stored on your iPhone with the photos.
  • PDF reports and ZIP backups: both are created on your iPhone. A ZIP backup contains your log as JSON and CSV files plus your photos. Lullog never uploads them; they leave your phone only if you send or save them yourself through the iOS share sheet, and then they go only where you choose.
  • Deleting: Settings → Delete all data removes growth entries, supply list items and photo tags from your iPhone. Files you have already saved or sent elsewhere are not affected.

Notifications

  • Reminders: optional next-feed reminders (with quiet hours) and sleep-timer checks are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are off until you turn them on, and nothing about them is sent to a server.
  • Supply alerts (Lullog Plus): when someone with a link marks a supply Low or Out, Lullog shows a local notification on your iPhone. It is created by the app when it syncs with the relay (when Lullog is opened or open), not sent as a push notification. Lullog does not register your device for push notifications.
  • iOS asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder or alert. You can turn them off in Lullog or in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Lullog Lifetime, Lullog Plus and the upgrade from Lifetime to Plus are one-time purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Settings → Delete all data removes every entry, every photo and caption, growth entries, supply list items, your baby's details, and all settings. Deleting the app does the same.
  • On the relay: Settings → Share with caregivers → Stop sharing → Stop sharing and delete from server permanently deletes the shared copy of your log, the shared supply list, every link, and your baby's details from our server, immediately. Settings → Delete all data does the same before erasing your phone. If you no longer have the app, email us with the share link and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Usage counts: turning off Settings → Share anonymous usage stats stops sending and deletes any counts still waiting on your phone. Raw events already on the server are removed within 30 days; after that only totals with no install ID remain.

Children

Lullog is used by parents and caregivers, not by children. The log describes an infant, but the app collects no information from the infant and no information that identifies the child beyond the name you choose to enter.

Changes

If a future version collects anything beyond what is described here, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Privacy details on the App Store will match it.
